## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Portable switch-transition pass

Oscillux SHALL provide one portable GLSL source defining `vec3 transition(int style, vec2 uv,
TransitionInputs t)` for the styles Luma Key, Photo Key, Melt, Downbeat Cut + Light Leak and
Beat Iris, returning linear HDR, with progress `p` taken as already eased by the host.

#### Scenario: Endpoints

- **WHEN** a style other than Downbeat Cut + Light Leak is evaluated at `p = 0`
- **THEN** it returns the outgoing colour, and at `p = 1` the incoming colour

#### Scenario: Downbeat cut

- **WHEN** Downbeat Cut + Light Leak is evaluated at `p = 0` or `p = 1`
- **THEN** it returns the incoming colour

### Requirement: Bounded added light

Every style SHALL add, per pixel, at most 0.04 of luminance-weighted positive linear RGB over its
plain reference (dissolve for Luma Key, Photo Key and Melt; the cut for Downbeat Cut + Light
Leak; the same aperture without rim for Beat Iris), so that after the ACES fitted tone map
(maximum slope 1.80) the added cell luminance stays at or below 0.08.

#### Scenario: Vectors respect the cap

- **WHEN** the vector model writes the golden outputs
- **THEN** it fails if any output exceeds the per-pixel bound or the tone-mapped 0.08 cap

### Requirement: Motion-free behaviour

With `motion = 0`, every style SHALL still meet its endpoints and the added-light bound, and Luma
Key and Photo Key SHALL equal the plain dissolve `mix(from, to, p)`.

#### Scenario: Luma Key without motion

- **WHEN** Luma Key is evaluated with `motion = 0`
- **THEN** its output equals `mix(from, to, p)` within the vector tolerance

### Requirement: Golden vectors

The repository SHALL hold 8x8 golden vectors for every style at `p` in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1},
regenerated byte for byte by their model, and `make gate` SHALL render the shader on a GPU
harness and compare it to them within the stated tolerance.

#### Scenario: Planted change

- **WHEN** one style's math is changed in the shader
- **THEN** the switch-transition check fails
