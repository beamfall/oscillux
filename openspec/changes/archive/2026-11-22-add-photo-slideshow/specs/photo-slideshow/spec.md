## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Portable photo-slideshow pass

Oscillux SHALL provide one portable GLSL source defining `vec3 slideshow(vec2 uv,
SlideshowInputs s)` that draws an outgoing and an incoming slide, each in the cover, blurred
fill or portrait pair layout, joined by the dissolve, light sweep or iris transition, returning
linear HDR, with transition progress `p` taken as already eased by the host.

#### Scenario: Endpoints

- **WHEN** any transition is evaluated at `p = 0`
- **THEN** it returns the outgoing slide, and at `p = 1` the incoming slide

#### Scenario: No bars

- **WHEN** a photo whose aspect differs from the frame is drawn in the fill or pair layout
- **THEN** every pixel shows the photo, its blurred backdrop or the pair gutter, never a bar

### Requirement: Ken Burns aimed at the salient rect

Each slide's window SHALL move linearly in centre and half size between a full window that
keeps the supplied salient rect in view and a window zoomed toward the rect's centre by at most
1.18, so every window on the path keeps the rect in view, and `motion = 0` SHALL hold the photo
still.

#### Scenario: Still at motion 0

- **WHEN** a slide is evaluated with `motion = 0` at two Ken Burns progress values
- **THEN** both outputs are equal

#### Scenario: Rect larger than the full window

- **WHEN** the salient rect, with its margin, is wider or taller than the layout's full window
- **THEN** on each axis the rect overflows, the window centres on the rect's centre as far as
  the photo's bounds allow, and the host contract tells hosts to choose the fill layout for that
  photo

#### Scenario: Iris centre ignores tempo coupling

- **WHEN** an iris transition is evaluated at amplitude 1 with two different beat phases
- **THEN** the aperture's centre is the same in both

### Requirement: Calm audio coupling

Audio SHALL move nothing unless `amplitude > 0`, and then only the pace of the Ken Burns move,
from the beat phase and each slide's own beat count over its time on screen (`beatPhase`, `beats`):
the move SHALL never reverse, its endpoints SHALL be unchanged and no light SHALL be added.

#### Scenario: Tempo coupling never reverses the move

- **WHEN** the path is followed over a whole slide at `amplitude = 1` for any slide `beats`
  of at least 1
- **THEN** it strictly increases and ends where it would without audio

#### Scenario: Each slide paces on its own beat count

- **WHEN** the outgoing and incoming slides have different times on screen
- **THEN** each slide's path uses the beat count the host supplies for that slide

#### Scenario: Amplitude 0 ignores audio

- **WHEN** the pass is evaluated with `amplitude = 0` and loud audio inputs
- **THEN** its output equals the output with silent audio inputs

### Requirement: Bounded added light

The light sweep SHALL add, per pixel, at most 0.04 of luminance-weighted positive linear RGB over
the same wipe without its band, and the dissolve and iris SHALL add none, so that after the ACES
fitted tone map the added cell luminance stays at or below 0.08.

#### Scenario: Loud-audio sequence under the limiter

- **WHEN** a worst-case slideshow sequence with loud audio and amplitude 1 is tone mapped and
  run through the limiter model and audit
- **THEN** the limiter's output makes at most six large-area luminance transitions and one
  large-area red transition in any second

### Requirement: Golden vectors

The repository SHALL hold 8x8 golden vectors for every transition at `p` in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75,
1} over every layout, regenerated byte for byte by their model, and `make gate` SHALL render the
pass on a GPU harness and compare it to them within the stated tolerance.

#### Scenario: Planted change

- **WHEN** one transition's math is changed in the shader
- **THEN** the slideshow check fails
